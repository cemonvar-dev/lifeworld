Security

Last updated 2 Aug 2026

A plain-language summary of how Fiblia protects your account and data.

Authentication

You sign in with your Google account. Fiblia only requests your name and email address — nothing else. We never see or store your Google password; authentication is handled by Google and Supabase using industry-standard OAuth.

Your data

Your data is stored with Supabase (a managed PostgreSQL platform). It’s transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest by our cloud provider. Row-Level Security is enforced so that no user can access another user's data. Authorized Fiblia operators and our providers can access data only as needed to run and support the service.

Payments

Payments and subscriptions are processed by Lemon Squeezy. Fiblia does not receive or store your card details.

Hosting

Fiblia is served over HTTPS and hosted on Vercel’s cloud infrastructure.

Responsible disclosure

If you believe you’ve found a security vulnerability, please email info@fiblia.com with the details and steps to reproduce. We’ll investigate and respond. Please give us a reasonable chance to address the issue before disclosing it publicly.
