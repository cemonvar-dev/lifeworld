About Fiblia

Last updated 2 Aug 2026

Fiblia is an independent product focused on one thing: helping you organize everything that matters, in one calm place.

What Fiblia is

Life is spread across habits, projects, people, and plans. Fiblia brings them together as simple “tiles” you can track and reflect on — without the noise of a heavyweight productivity suite.

What you can do

  • Capture anything as a tile — habits, projects, one-off tasks.
  • See what’s due and log what you did on a calendar.
  • Filter your world by tag or mood.
  • Ask an AI assistant about your tasks, by text or voice.
  • Turn intentions into a longer-term plan.
  • Get gentle, on-device reminders.

Independent by design

Fiblia is built and run independently. Our aim is a calm, focused tool you can trust — see our Security page for how we handle authentication, your data, and payments.
