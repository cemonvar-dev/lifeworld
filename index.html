The Aim

Replace discipline-only habit tracking with a living world that rewards identity, balance, and self-awareness.

🧭

The Goal

Help you return consistently because it feels inviting — progress becomes visual, emotional, and satisfying.

The Perspective

No decay. No shame. When you slow down, your world shifts seasons — and when you act again, it blooms back.

How it works (simple)

  • Log an action
    A small real-life step updates a tile/region in your world.
  • See the weather
    Engagement patterns become sunshine, clouds, rain, fog — not punishment.
  • Come back gently
    Long gaps don’t destroy progress. They only change the mood until you return.
